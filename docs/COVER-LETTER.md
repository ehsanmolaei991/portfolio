# Cover Letter

A reusable template plus a filled example. **Before sending:** fill every `{{...}}`
and `[...]` token — never submit the raw template (an ATS reads placeholders as spam).
Keep the cover letter *out* of your résumé PDF; send it as the email body or a separate file.

---

## Reusable Cover Letter Template

> Fill `{{Company}}`, `{{Role}}`, `{{team_or_product}}`, `{{why_company}}`, plus the bracketed `[...]` fields. Keep it to 3 paragraphs, ≤ 250 words.

---
